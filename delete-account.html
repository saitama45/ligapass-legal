Delete your LigaPass account

In the app

  1. Open LigaPass and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password, and confirm.

Your phone needs a connection. Anything you did on it that has not reached the cloud yet is sent first.

By email

If you cannot use the app, write to gmcloud45@gmail.com from the email address your account signs in with, with the subject Delete my LigaPass account. We confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your email address and mobile number, your profile photo, and how to reach you on any team you played for. Nobody can sign in as your account again.
  • Kept: the leagues' records of the games you played — scores and statistics, under the name on the roster — and the date of birth a league checked your eligibility with. They belong to no account any more, and are kept as part of those leagues' results and standings, for as long as the league's organization uses LigaPass. In a league's record of actions your account appears as "Deleted account".

Deleting cannot be undone. If your account owns an organization, hand the organization over to someone else first (or ask us to).
