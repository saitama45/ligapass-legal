LigaPass Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

LigaPass helps basketball leagues run their seasons: organizations, leagues, teams and rosters, schedules, courtside scoring, results and statistics, and each player's history of games. This policy says what the app collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and how to have it deleted.

Who sees it

  • The people of your leagues. A league's people — its organization's owner and staff, its commissioners, scorers and team managers — see its teams, rosters, games, results and statistics. Players see the leagues they play in.
  • Contact details are narrower. A player's email address, mobile number and date of birth are seen only by the people who run their league, the manager of their team, whoever added them, and the player themself.
  • Organizations are kept apart. Another organization's people see none of your organization's teams, players or records.
  • The services that run LigaPass process data on our behalf only: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Singapore) and Google (Gmail, which delivers the app's emails). They may not use it for anything else.
  • Paying online. When you pay for credits online you pay on the page of PayMongo, our payment provider, which handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We send PayMongo only what is being bought and its price — not your name or contact details — and PayMongo tells us whether the payment went through.
  • Live streams. A game a league streams is seen by whoever the streaming service shows it to — on Facebook, the audience the league's page or profile chooses — under that service's own terms and privacy policy. The league decides whether to stream, and should tell everyone at the game that it is streamed.
  • We disclose information when the law requires it.

How it is protected

Connections to our backend and mail services use HTTPS/TLS. Live streams use the server address chosen by the league: RTMPS encrypts the video, sound and stream key; RTMP does not. Use RTMPS when streaming over the internet. Every table in our database is closed by default and opened only by rules that decide, row by row, who may read or change it. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, and your sign-in in the phone's secure storage.

How long it is kept

Your account is kept until you delete it. A league's records — rosters, games, results, statistics and its record of actions — are kept for as long as the league's organization uses LigaPass, because they are that league's history.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete my account, with your password. You can also ask us by email (see below). When your account is deleted:

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your email address and mobile number, your profile photo, and how to reach you on any team you played for.
  • Kept, belonging to no account: the leagues' records of games you played, under the name on the roster, and the date of birth a league checked your eligibility with — they are part of those leagues' results and standings. In the record of actions, your account appears as "Deleted account".

Deleting cannot be undone. An account that owns an organization is handed over to someone else first. Details: Delete your LigaPass account.

Children

LigaPass is made for the people who run basketball leagues and the players in them. It is not directed at children under 13.

  • Accounts. You must be at least 13 years old to create a LigaPass account. If you are under 18, you need the permission of a parent or guardian to create one.
  • Players on rosters. Youth leagues may put players of any age on a team's roster; those players do not need an account. A league that adds a player under 18 is responsible for having the consent of that player's parent or guardian to record the player's details and games — and, where it streams their games live, to show them on camera.
  • Parents and guardians may ask us to see, correct or delete their child's information by writing to gmcloud45@gmail.com. We may ask for proof that you are the child's parent or guardian.
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we delete that account.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask to be told what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted, and you may object to its processing. To do so, or for any question about this policy, write to gmcloud45@gmail.com. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.
